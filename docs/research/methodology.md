# Research methodology

Status: working protocol · Prepared 2026-11-09.

## Question and scope

How do accessible, heritage, and premium essentials competitors communicate the value of a plain white T-shirt? Compare one named product per brand in the US/USD context. Three purposively selected examples are exploratory, not a representative market survey. They are multi-product brands; later research should add verified single-product businesses.

## Evidence rules

- **User-reported:** supplied in the assignment; original observation date and capture unavailable.
- **Source-observed:** visible in an official page or indexed excerpt checked on the recorded date. This confirms what a brand says, not product performance.
- **Hypothesis:** researcher interpretation requiring testing; never a brand's declared intent.
- **Unverified / pending:** evidence absent, variant mismatch, or capture incomplete. Unknown is not zero.
- **Conflicting:** retain both observations; resolve SKU, date, currency, and color before using as a fact.

## Repeatable collection

1. Record full URL, access date, country/currency, product ID/version, selected color, size, and regular versus sale price.
2. Capture desktop and mobile screenshots with viewport, date, selected variant, and filename. Avoid personal information. Keep screenshots as evidence, with descriptive alt text and source attribution.
3. Transcribe only supported facts: material, weight, fit, measurements, claims, price, reviews, shipping, returns. Attribute marketing claims; do not infer durability, ethics, opacity, or sustainability from price.
4. Separate interface observation from interpretation. Compare navigation, hierarchy, type, spacing, images, information density, and mobile behavior using equivalent viewports.
5. Apply archetype and design lenses with a supporting cue and alternative explanation. Record Cialdini cues only when present; mark all others unobserved.
6. Update profile, evidence register, and matrix together. Recheck prices before presentation and retain prior observations in Git history.

## Analysis lenses

Archetypes are interpretive storytelling categories, not personality measurements. Modernist observations concern functional hierarchy, restraint, and clarity; postmodernist observations concern quotation, eclecticism, irony, or layered cultural meaning. A product description alone cannot establish a visual movement.

Use Cialdini's reciprocity, commitment/consistency, social proof, authority, liking, scarcity, and unity as questions, not evidence of effectiveness. See [ethical synthesis](./synthesis.md).

## Limits and validation

No garment purchases, wash tests, interviews, or usability sessions have occurred. Browser-rendered visual inspection and original screenshot capture are pending. Official product pages are commercial sources; search excerpts may lag. Validate positioning with consenting participants, avoid leading questions, and anonymize notes. Compare an identical white/off-white category carefully; do not rank quality using price alone.
